## 📌 Overview

**Kurina Paper Soap** is an empirical research project developing single-use, biodegradable paper soap sheets synthesized from local agricultural waste in Kudus Regency: **Karmilin banana midribs** (*Musa paradisiaca Linn.*) and **Rambutan leaves** (*Nephelium lappaceum L.*), compounded with purified cosmetic-grade **Kaolin clay** (SiO2 46.54%, Al2O3 39.50%).

The paper soap is engineered to eliminate cross-contamination risks common in shared public restrooms while addressing the need for practical, hygienic purification (including ritual cleansing standards for heavy impurities without carrying raw outdoor soil).

## 🔬 Analytical & Experimental Results

### 1. Gas Chromatography–Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS @ Lab Terpadu UNDIP)
Spectrometric testing of the ethanolic extract of Karmilin banana midribs at the Integrated Laboratory Unit of Diponegoro University (UNDIP) confirmed 5 dominant bioactive compounds:

| Peak | Compound Name | Retention Time (min) | Area (%) | Functional Role |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **9-Tetradecenal, (Z)-** | 20.841 | **28.42%** | Antiseborrheic and skin barrier protection |
| **2** | **Hexadecanoic acid (Palmitic acid)** | 25.992 | **25.69%** | Natural surfactant and lipid cleansing agent |
| **3** | **9-Octadecenoic acid (Z)-, ethyl ester** | 29.351 | **17.14%** | Gram-positive antibacterial membrane inhibitor |
| **4** | **Dodecanoic acid, ethyl ester** | 29.989 | **7.49%** | Foaming stabilizer and cosmetic emollient |
| **5** | **Octadecanoic acid, ethyl ester** | 33.851 | **5.54%** | Skin conditioning and texture stabilizer |

### 2. Phytochemical Screening of Rambutan Leaves (*Nephelium lappaceum L.*)
* **Saponins:** Positive (Stable dense foam > 30 seconds).
* **Flavonoids:** Positive (Intense orange-red reaction upon Mg + HCl addition).
* **Polyphenols:** Positive (Intense dark black precipitation with FeCl3 10%).
* **Tannins:** Positive (Condensed tannin greenish-black precipitation).
* **Steroids:** Positive (Liebermann-Burchard violet/green interfacial ring).

### 3. Antibacterial Disk Diffusion Assay (*Staphylococcus aureus*)
Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion on Nutrient Agar medium showed significant inhibition:

| Sample Group | Concentration | Inhibition Diameter (mm) | Activity Category |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Kurina Paper Soap** | **100 ppm** | **7.4 mm** | **Moderate (3.5x Control)** |
| Commercial Antibacterial Soap | Standard | 2.1 mm | Weak |
| Distilled Water (Negative Control) | Blank | 0.0 mm | Inactive |

## 🧼 Physical Formulation & Standards

* **Form Factor:** 4.5 cm x 7.0 cm single-use biodegradable sheets.
* **pH Level:** Stabilized at pH 10.0, conforming to Indonesian National Standard **SNI 2588:2017** for cleansing soap.
* **Functional Cleansing:** Incorporates Kaolin clay (hydrated aluminum disilicate) to meet soil-contact cleansing standards safely and hygienically.
* **Sensory Evaluation (N = 25):** Mean score of 4.31/5.00 for practicality and 3.81/5.00 for cleaning efficacy.
