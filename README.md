## 📚 Flagship Research Papers in this Repository

### 1. 🌿 [Kurina Paper Soap: Biomedical Phytotherapy & Islamic Thaharah Jurisprudence](01-kurina-paper-soap-biomedical-thaharah/)
* **Full Title:** *Kurina Paper Soap: Inovasi Sabun Kertas dengan Ekstrak Pelepah Daun Pisang Karmilin dan Daun Rambutan sebagai Sabun Pensuci Najis sekaligus Penghambat Bakteri Staphylococcus aureus*
* **Authors:** Muhammad Arjun Nabila & Erix Misbahuddin Tri Saputro
* **Milestone:** Senior Thesis Defense (Tugas Akhir Madrasah 2023/2024) & Undip Biotechnology Scientific Competition Selection.
* **Key Findings:**
  * **Gas Chromatography–Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) @ UPT Lab Terpadu UNDIP:** Quantified five major active compounds, with *9-Tetradecenal, (Z)-* (**28.42%**) and *Hexadecanoic acid* (**25.69%**).
  * **In Vitro Antibacterial Efficacy:** Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion demonstrated a **7.4 mm inhibition zone** against pathogenic *Staphylococcus aureus* at 100 ppm, outperforming conventional commercial antibacterial soap (2.1 mm) by **3.5x**.
  * **Islamic Legal Harmonization:** Formulated with cosmetic-grade **Kaolin clay** (SiO2 46.54%, Al2O3 39.50%), providing a hygienic, medically safe vehicle for *najis mughalladhah* ritual purification conforming to Fatwa Samak/Sertu 2011.
  * **Standard Compliance:** Formulated at pH 10.0, fulfilling Indonesian National Standard **SNI 2588:2017**.
* **Direct Access:** [Read Technical Documentation & Datasets](01-kurina-paper-soap-biomedical-thaharah/)

### 2. ☕ [Green Synthesis of Fluorescent Carbon Dots from Spent Coffee Grounds](02-nanotechnology-coffee-waste-carbon-dots/)
* **Full Title:** *Sintesis Carbon Dots (C-Dots) dari Ampas Kopi (Coffea) Menggunakan Metode Microwave sebagai Pendeteksi Logam Berat Merkuri (Hg)*
* **Authors:** Erix Misbahuddin Tri Saputro & Muhammad Arjun Nabila
* **Key Highlights:**
  * Investigates green, energy-efficient microwave-assisted pyrolysis of post-consumer coffee waste into zero-dimensional fluorescent Carbon Quantum Dots (diameter < 10 nm).
  * Demonstrates selective fluorescence quenching of surface functional states (C=O, C-OH) when interacting with toxic divalent mercury ions (Hg2+) for optical sensing in water purification.
* **Direct Access:** [Read Documentation & Manuscript](02-nanotechnology-coffee-waste-carbon-dots/)

### 3. 🧼 [Sodium Bicarbonate (NaHCO3) as a Natural Physical Exfoliant](03-baking-soda-dermatology-exfoliant/)
* **Full Title:** *Identifikasi Kandungan pada Baking Soda (Natrium Bikarbonat) sebagai Eksfoliator Alami dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Bakteri Penyebab Jerawat Propionibacterium acnes*
* **Authors:** Erix Misbahuddin Tri Saputro & Muhammad Arjun Nabila
* **Key Highlights:**
  * Evaluates stoichiometric dissociation, micro-crystal geometry, and buffering kinetics of NaHCO3 on facial epidermal integrity.
  * Analyzes antibacterial susceptibility of *Cutibacterium acnes* (formerly *Propionibacterium acnes*) under buffered pH conditions to establish clinical safety margins.
* **Direct Access:** [Read Documentation & Manuscript](03-baking-soda-dermatology-exfoliant/)
