## 📌 Abstract (English & Bahasa Indonesia)

### English
Rapid urban mobility poses distinct challenges for personal hygiene, healthcare barrier maintenance, and religious ritual purity (*thaharah*) according to Islamic jurisprudence. Conventional bar soaps in shared environments carry inherent risks of microbial cross-contamination. Furthermore, practicing Muslims face logistical bottlenecks when executing ritual purification from heavy impurities (*najis mughalladhah*), which strictly mandates seven washings including one contact with clean soil or clay minerals. 

This research engineers **Kurina Paper Soap**, an innovative, single-use, biodegradable paper soap formulated from agro-industrial botanical waste: **Karmilin banana midribs** (*Musa paradisiaca Linn.*) and **Rambutan leaves** (*Nephelium lappaceum L.*), compounded with pure cosmetic-grade **Kaolin clay** (SiO2 46.54%, Al2O3 39.50%). Analytical Gas Chromatography–Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) performed at UPT Laboratorium Terpadu Universitas Diponegoro (UNDIP) identified five dominant secondary bioactive compounds, headed by *9-Tetradecenal, (Z)-* (28.42%) and *Hexadecanoic acid* (25.69%). In vitro Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion against the pathogenic bacterium *Staphylococcus aureus* demonstrated a **7.4 mm inhibition zone** at 100 ppm, outperforming standard commercial antibacterial soap (2.1 mm) by 3.5x. Chemical characterization confirmed optimal formulation pH at 10 (conforming to SNI 2588:2017 standards).

### Bahasa Indonesia
Mobilitas masyarakat modern membutuhkan sediaan pembersih yang higienis, praktis, dan mampu menjawab kebutuhan syariat terkait pensucian najis mughalladhah (anjing/babi) tanpa repot membawa tanah konvensional. Penelitian ini memformulasikan sabun kertas sekali pakai berbahan ekstrak limbah pelepah pisang karmilin lokal Kudus dan daun rambutan, dipadukan dengan mineral lempung kaolin murni. Pengujian GC-MS di Lab Terpadu UNDIP Semarang membuktikan keberadaan senyawa aktif antiseboroik dan antimikroba alami. Uji efikasi antibakteri terhadap *Staphylococcus aureus* menunjukkan zona hambat 7.4 mm (3.5 kali lebih luas dibanding sabun komersial). Sediaan lolos uji standar SNI 2588:2017 dan mendapat respons penerimaan sangat positif pada uji organoleptik.

## 🔬 Scientific Methodology & Analytical Pipelines

### 1. Extraction Protocol (Maceration)
* **Karmilin Banana Midribs (*Musa paradisiaca Linn.*):** Collected from Gebog, Kudus Regency. Washed, diced, dried, and macerated in ethanol solvent (96%) for 72 hours, filtered, and concentrated via rotary evaporator.
* **Rambutan Leaves (*Nephelium lappaceum L.*):** Collected from Kaliwungu, Kudus Regency. Macerated using 90% ethanol to isolate polar and semi-polar secondary metabolites.

### 2. Analytical Instrumentation (GC-MS @ UNDIP Semarang)
Spectrometric identification was conducted at the Integrated Laboratory Unit of Diponegoro University (UPT Lab Terpadu UNDIP) using a Gas Chromatography–Mass Spectrometer with helium carrier gas.

## ⚖️ Islamic Sharia Jurisprudence Framework (*Fikih Thaharah*)

### 1. Legal Basis for Soil/Clay in Najis Mughalladhah
Under the Shafi'i and Hanbali schools of Islamic jurisprudence (*madhhab*), cleansing from contact with canine or porcine contamination requires washing seven times, with one wash incorporating clean earth/soil (*turab thahur*):
> *"The purification of the vessel of one of you, when a dog has licked in it, is that he should wash it seven times, the first of which is with earth."* (Sahih Muslim, No. 279).

### 2. Mineralogical Justification of Kaolin Clay
Raw soil carries pathogens (parasite eggs, spores, environmental bacteria). Kurina Paper Soap solves this through cosmetic-grade **Kaolin Clay**:
* **Chemical Composition:** Hydrated aluminum disilicate (**SiO2 46.54%**, **Al2O3 39.50%**).
* **Syariah Compliance:** Conforms to the ruling of the **Majlis Kebangsaan Hal Ehwal Ugama Islam Malaysia (Fatwa Samak/Sertu 2011)** and scholars' consensus permitting purified clay containing natural mineral earth for ritual ablutions and thaharah.
* **Mechanism:** Acts as an adsorbent binding to polar toxins while meeting the scriptural mandate of soil-element contact.
