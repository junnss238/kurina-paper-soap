## 📌 Executive Summary
Acne vulgaris, primarily driven by colonization of *Propionibacterium acnes* (now *Cutibacterium acnes*) and follicular hyperkeratinization, remains a prevalent dermatological concern. While sodium bicarbonate (baking soda, NaHCO3) is widely used in folk skincare regimens for chemical peeling and comedone reduction, unmoderated alkaline exposure risks disrupting the epidermal acid mantle.

### Key Highlights
* **Chemical Profile & Buffering Capacity:** Investigates stoichiometric dissociation, micro-abrasive crystal geometry, and safe concentration thresholds for topical application.
* **In Vitro Antimicrobial Screening:** Evaluates bactericidal and bacteriostatic properties against *P. acnes* cultures under controlled pH environments.
* **Formulation Guidelines:** Identifies risk parameters and optimal dosage buffers to mitigate cutaneous irritation while maintaining mild keratolytic benefits.
