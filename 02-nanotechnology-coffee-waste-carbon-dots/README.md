## 📌 Executive Summary
Carbon Quantum Dots (C-Dots) are fluorescent zero-dimensional carbon nanomaterials (diameter < 10 nm) characterized by low cytotoxicity, high water dispersibility, stable photoluminescence, and biocompatibility. Conventional quantum dot sensors rely on toxic semiconductor materials (e.g., CdTe, ZnS). This study explores a **green chemistry paradigm**, utilizing agro-waste **spent coffee grounds** (*Coffea*) as an abundant, eco-friendly carbon precursor.

### Key Highlights
* **Facile Microwave-Assisted Pyrolysis:** Rapid, energy-efficient bottom-up synthesis optimizing carbonization temperature and exposure duration.
* **Photoluminescence Quenching Mechanism:** Exploits electron/energy transfer between functionalized surface states (C=O, C-OH) on C-Dots and toxic heavy metal ions (Hg2+), resulting in fluorescence quenching for optical biosensing.
* **Environmental Impact:** Repurposes organic cafe waste into high-value analytical sensors for toxic industrial effluent monitoring.
